How It Works: The Booking Flow

We build a system that works tirelessly in the background, filling your calendar with qualified clients.

1. Share Your Link

Embed your custom booking page directly on your website or share your unique link via email, social media, or WhatsApp.

2. Client Selects Time

Clients view only the time slots you have approved. They select a time, fill out custom intake questions, and pay the required fee.

3. Instant Automation

The appointment is instantly added to your calendar. A unique Zoom link is generated, and a confirmation email is sent to the client.

4. Meet & Convert

Automated reminders ensure they show up on time. You simply log in, review their intake form, and conduct a successful meeting.
