The Smart Billing Workflow

We build a pipeline that takes you from project completion to cash-in-bank with zero friction.

1. Generate

Create professional invoices in seconds from pre-filled templates, or have them generated automatically based on CRM deals or time-tracking tools.

2. Send & Notify

Invoices are emailed to clients with a secure, embedded "Pay Now" button, allowing them to settle the bill via Card, ACH, or UPI instantly.

3. Track & Remind

The system tracks when the client opens the invoice. If unpaid, polite, automated follow-up sequences are triggered before and after the due date.

4. Paid & Synced

Funds hit your bank account. The invoice is marked as paid, a receipt is sent to the client, and the data is automatically synced to your accounting software.
