Our Campaign Execution Strategy

A proven, data-backed approach to ensuring your emails land in the primary inbox, not the spam folder.

1. Audit & Strategy

We analyze your current list health, past campaign performance, and deliverability rates. Then, we map out a 90-day content calendar and automation roadmap.

2. Copy & Design

Our creative team drafts persuasive subject lines and email body copy, while designers build custom, brand-aligned templates that look perfect on desktop and mobile.

3. Technical Setup

We handle the complex backend work—setting up DKIM/SPF records for deliverability, integrating with your CRM/E-commerce store, and building the automation logic.

4. Monitor & Optimize

Once live, we don't just walk away. We continuously monitor open rates, clean out unengaged subscribers, and run A/B tests to squeeze out every drop of ROI.
