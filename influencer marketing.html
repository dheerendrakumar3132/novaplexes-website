Our Campaign Execution Strategy

We eliminate the chaos of managing influencers with a streamlined, transparent process.

1. Strategy & Briefing

We define the campaign goal (Awareness vs. Sales), establish KPIs, and draft a strict creative brief that outlines brand guidelines and key talking points.

2. Scouting & Vetting

Using our proprietary tools, we curate a list of influencers. We manually audit their accounts for fake followers, engagement quality, and brand safety.

3. Negotiation & Contracts

We handle all the legal paperwork, ensuring you have the rights to use their content for ads (UGC Rights), and negotiate the best possible rates.

4. Execution & Analytics

Content goes live! We monitor the comments, track the affiliate links, and provide you with a comprehensive post-campaign report detailing your ROI.
