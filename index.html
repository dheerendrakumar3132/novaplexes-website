Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Novaplexes.

What makes Novaplexes different from other agencies?

Unlike traditional agencies that only focus on one aspect, we provide a holistic approach. We combine custom Web Development with advanced AI integrations, Business Automation, and aggressive Marketing to ensure your business scales systematically.

Do you work with startups or established brands?

We work with both. For startups, we focus on aggressive growth, branding, and establishing a digital footprint. For established brands, we focus on automation, CRO (Conversion Rate Optimization), and scaling ad spend profitably.

How long does it take to see SEO results?

While web development and advertising can yield immediate results, SEO is a long-term game. Typically, our clients start seeing significant jumps in organic traffic and keyword rankings between month 3 and month 6.

What technologies do you use for Web Development?

We utilize a modern tech stack. Depending on the project, we use pure HTML/CSS/JS, React.js, Node.js, WordPress for content-heavy sites, and Shopify for E-commerce platforms.
